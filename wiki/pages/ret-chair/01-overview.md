# RET Chair — Overview

The RET Chair handles **Research, Extension & Training**: configuring the rank-based rules,
assigning/eligibility, and reviewing RET submissions. A RET Chair is also a **designated
faculty member** with their own IPCR.

![RET Chair dashboard — Overview](/static/img/ret-chair/00-overview.png)

## The sidebar

| Group | Item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | **Overview** | Registered targets, configured ranks, pending approvals |
| Phases | **Cascaded Targets** | The RET quotas the Dean cascaded |
| Phases | **Target Assignment** | Assign RET targets to faculty |
| Phases | **Menu Config** | Configure the rank-based Research/Extension rules |
| Phases | **Commitments** | Review RET submissions |
| Verification | **Evidence Monitor** | Monitor RET evidence |
| My Performance | **My IPCR** | Your own IPCR |

## Reading the Overview

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Registered Targets** | Active DPCR research targets |
| **Configured Ranks** | Active rank-based rules set |
| **Pending Approvals** | Faculty selections awaiting your review |

## How RET differs from the Program Chair

- **Research** is **self-selected** from a pool you configure, with a quantity per rank band.
- **Extension** is **distributed** — auto-flows to faculty based on your rules; it is
  mandatory rather than self-selected.
- Research/Extension targets route to you (review lane **RET**), not to the Program Chair.

## Work order

1. **Menu Config** — set the rank-based rules (which research a rank may pick, extension
   mandatory quantities).
2. **Cascaded Targets** — review what the Dean cascaded to RET.
3. **Target Assignment** — assign/confirm faculty targets.
4. **Commitments** and **Evidence Monitor** — review and verify.
