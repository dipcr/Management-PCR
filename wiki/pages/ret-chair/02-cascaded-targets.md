# RET Chair — Cascaded Targets

Shows the **RET / Extension** quotas the Dean cascaded. These are the totals you will
distribute or pool across the faculty in your sector.

![Cascaded Targets](/static/img/ret-chair/02-cascaded-targets.png)

## What you see

For each indicator cascaded to **RET / Extension**:

- the **master indicator** description
- the **Dean's quota** for RET
- how much has been **distributed** so far

## Why the quota exists

The Dean cascades a single RET quota per indicator. You then either:

- **distribute** it (Extension — auto-flows per your rules), or
- expose it as a **self-selection pool** at a quantity-per-rank (Research).

If this screen is empty, the Dean has not cascaded any RET/Extension quotas for the active
term yet.

## Next step

Go to **Menu Config** to define the rank-based rules that determine how these quotas reach
faculty, then **Target Assignment** to apply them.
