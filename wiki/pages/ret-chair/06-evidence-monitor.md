# RET Chair — Evidence Monitor

Monitor the evidence faculty submit for **Research and Extension** committed targets, and
verify it as part of the RET review lane.

![Evidence Monitor](/static/img/ret-chair/06-evidence-monitor.png)

## Using the monitor

1. Go to **Evidence Monitor**.
2. Filter to the faculty member or target you want to check.
3. Inspect the uploaded PDF evidence and the claimed accomplishment.
4. **Approve** to verify, or **Return** with a comment.

## How this fits the pipeline

- Evidence is uploaded **per committed target** after the IPCR is locked.
- RET evidence is verified here; Instruction/Support evidence is verified by the Program
  Chair.
- A faculty member with **no RET evidence** skips this step entirely.
- Verified evidence feeds the **scoring** phase.

## Tips

- Watch the **Evidence Readiness** status on the faculty side so you know who is waiting on
  a RET verification.
- Returned evidence keeps the target open until the faculty member re-uploads.
