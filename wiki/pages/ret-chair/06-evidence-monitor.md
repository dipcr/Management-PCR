# RET Chair — Evidence Monitor

A **read-only monitor**. It lists regular faculty with Research & Extension committed targets
**whose evidence the Program Chair has already fully approved** — RET targets included. You
do not verify evidence here, so there is nothing for you to approve.

![Evidence Monitor](/static/img/ret-chair/06-evidence-monitor.png)

## Using the monitor

1. Go to **Evidence Monitor**.
2. Search or scroll to the faculty member you want to check.
3. Read their committed RET targets, how many were met, and the verification status.
4. If something looks wrong, raise it with the **Program Chair** — they are the ones who
   approve the evidence.

## How this fits the pipeline

- Evidence is uploaded **per committed target** after the IPCR is locked.
- The **Program Chair approves all regular faculty evidence** — Research and Extension
  included. The **Dean** then signs off the package. (A Program Chair, RET Chair or the Dean's
  own evidence goes straight to the Dean, since nobody else can review it.)
- Only faculty whose evidence is **already approved** appear on this page, so the list holds
  nothing for you to action.
- Verified evidence feeds the **scoring** phase.

## Tips

- The list stays empty until the Program Chair finishes approving someone's evidence — an
  empty monitor early in the term is normal.
- Use it to answer "has this Research target been evidenced yet?" without leaving your
  dashboard.
