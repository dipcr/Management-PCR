# Program Chair — Evidence Verification

Verify the accomplishment evidence your faculty upload against their **committed targets**,
and monitor your department's progress.

![Evidence Verification](/static/img/prog-chair/04-evidence-verification.png)

## Departmental Accomplishment Summary

The first card compares the **quota cascaded to your program** against the accomplishment
**already approved by you**:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Departmental Oversight Target** | The cascaded target |
| **Quota** | The cascaded quantity |
| **Verified Accomplished** | Units already approved |
| **Progress** | Verified ÷ Quota |

## Verifying evidence

1. In **Faculty Evidence Submissions**, find the faculty member (or search by name).
2. Open their submitted evidence files (PDFs).
3. Check the claimed accomplishment quantity, then **Approve** or **Return** with a comment.

## The three lists

| List | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Faculty Evidence Submissions** | Evidence awaiting your verification |
| **Approved Regular Faculty Evidences** | Evidence you have already approved |
| **Departmental Accomplishment Summary** | Quota vs. approved accomplishment |

## Notes

- A faculty member with **no RET evidence at all** skips the RET verification step, so you can
  proceed immediately.
- Returned evidence keeps the target open; the faculty member re-uploads and the cycle
  repeats.
- Approved evidence feeds the **scoring** phase automatically.
