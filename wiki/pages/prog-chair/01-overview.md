# Program Chair — Overview

The Program Chair distributes the quotas the Dean cascaded, reviews faculty drafts, and
verifies evidence for their department. A Program Chair is also a **designated faculty
member** with their own IPCR.

![Program Chair dashboard — Overview](/static/img/prog-chair/00-overview.png)

## The sidebar

| Group | Item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | **Overview** | Faculty count, allocation progress, pending drafts |
| Phases | **Target Allocation** | Distribute Instruction & Support quotas to faculty |
| Phases | **Commitments** | Review faculty draft IPCRs |
| Verification | **Evidence Verification** | Verify uploaded evidence |
| My Performance | **My IPCR** | Your own IPCR (you are a designated faculty member) |

## Reading the Overview

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active Faculty** | Faculty under your specialization |
| **Target Allocation** | % of your cascaded quotas that have been distributed |
| **Pending Drafts** | Faculty drafts awaiting your review |
| **Approved IPCRs** | Faculty IPCRs approved this term |

## Your scope

Everything you see is scoped to **your specialization** (your department/program). You
distribute:

- **Instruction** targets to **all faculty** under your program
- **Support** targets to **regular faculty** (plus any College-Wide Support the Dean marked
  *To Chairs* — those carry a **College-Wide** badge)

## Work order

1. **Target Allocation** — distribute the Dean's quotas.
2. **Commitments** — review and approve/return faculty drafts.
3. **Evidence Verification** — verify evidence as it arrives.
