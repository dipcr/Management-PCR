# Program Chair — Target Allocation (Phase 1)

Distribute the Dean's cascaded quotas to the faculty in your department. This is **Phase 1:
Standard Target Allocation**.

![Phase 1 — Standard Target Allocation](/static/img/prog-chair/02-target-allocation.png)

## The two tables

| Table | Who receives it | Indicators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategic Priorities** | All faculty under your program | **Instruction** |
| **Support Functions** | Regular faculty (plus College-Wide Support) | **Support** |

Each row shows:

- **Master Indicator (DPCR Target)** — the target sentence
- **Dept Quota** — the number the Dean cascaded. A **College-Wide** badge means the Dean
  cascaded it college-wide and marked it *To Chairs* (decide your own per-faculty target;
  do not simply divide the college total by your faculty count)
- **Assigned Per Faculty** — the number each faculty member will receive
- **Deadline** — duration value + unit (days / weeks / months / semesters)
- **IPCR Target Description (Faculty Version)** — the wording each faculty member will see
- **Total Distributed** — *assigned × faculty count* vs. the quota

## Distributing

1. Optionally click **Auto Divide All Targets** (or **Auto Divide Section**) to divide each
   quota evenly by the relevant faculty count.
2. Or type an **Assigned Per Faculty** value per row and set each **Deadline**.
3. Watch **Total Distributed** against the quota so you do not over- or under-distribute.
4. Click **Save Targets**.

> Once saved, this baseline allocation is **finalized** and the inputs become read-only.

## Capacity reminder

You cannot save until every assigned target has a valid **deadline (duration)**. The counter
beside each section header tells you how many people that table feeds (*Number of Faculty* /
*Number of Regular Faculty*).

## "No targets assigned"

If both tables are empty, the Dean has not cascaded anything that matches your
specialization yet — or the quota was College-Wide **Instruction** (which never reaches the
chair). Ask the Dean to cascade to your department for Instruction/Support indicators.
