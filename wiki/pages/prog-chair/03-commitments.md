# Program Chair — Commitments (Phase 2)

Review the draft IPCRs your faculty submitted. This is **Phase 2: Commitment
Verification**.

![Phase 2 — Commitment Verification](/static/img/prog-chair/03-commitments.png)

## The two lists

| List | Contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending Draft Submissions** | Faculty drafts awaiting your review |
| **Approved & Locked IPCRs** | Faculty whose IPCRs are finalized/committed |

## Review a draft

1. Open a pending draft.
2. Review each target — quantity, target description, and duration (deadline).
3. **Adjust** quantities or add **remarks** where needed.
4. **Approve** the IPCR, or **Return** it to the faculty member with remarks.

## What "Returned" does

Returning a draft moves it back to the faculty member's **Draft** state so they can correct
it and resubmit. It re-enters your queue afterwards.

## What "Approved" does

An approved draft proceeds toward the Dean's **Final Verification**, then **locking** (where
its targets are copied to *committed targets* and evidence upload opens).

## Who appears here

Only faculty under **your specialization**. RET-eligible submissions (those with Research
targets) are reviewed by the **RET Chair first**, then land here; everything else comes
straight to you.
