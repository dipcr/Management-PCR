# D-IPCR User Manual

Welcome to the **D-IPCR** (Digital IPCR System) user manual and tutorial. This wiki walks
every role through the system **step by step**, using screenshots taken from the live app.

> **Live app:** https://dipcr.yaspartame.site

## The six roles

| Role | What they do | Manual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Opens terms, configures criteria & indicators, manages the roster, approves account claims, keeps the audit log | [Admin manual](/p/admin/01-overview) |
| **Dean** | Cascades college quotas, manages designated-faculty drafts, verifies evidence, final approval | [Dean manual](/p/dean/01-overview) |
| **Program Chair** | Distributes Instruction & Support quotas, reviews faculty drafts and evidence | [Program Chair manual](/p/prog-chair/01-overview) |
| **RET Chair** | Configures Research/Extension rules, assigns RET targets, monitors evidence | [RET Chair manual](/p/ret-chair/01-overview) |
| **Designated Faculty** | Authors their own IPCR through the shared designated flow (chairs and the Dean included) | [Designated Faculty manual](/p/designated-faculty/01-overview) |
| **Regular Faculty** | Assembles and submits a draft IPCR and uploads evidence | [Faculty manual](/p/faculty/01-overview) |

## How to use this wiki

- Use the **sidebar** to move between roles and screens.
- Use **Search** (top right) to find a screen, button or term.
- Every procedure is written as numbered steps; screenshots show the real app and the
  buttons you should click are named in **bold**.

## A note on terminology

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **IPCR** | Individual Performance Commitment and Review — the form each faculty member completes |
| **DPCR** | The department/college-level counterpart; the source of the master indicators |
| **Master Indicator** | A target definition created by the Admin for a term (e.g. "Submit *n* reports…") |
| **Cascade** | The Admin → Dean → Chair → Faculty flow that pushes quotas down to people |
| **Designated Faculty** | Anyone with an accountability role (Program Chair, RET Chair, Dean) — they each have their own IPCR too |
