# Admin — Master Indicators

Master Indicators are the actual targets for the term — the "DPCR targets" that cascade down
to people. Create them **after** the criteria and the term are set up.

![Master Indicators](/static/img/admin/06-master-indicators.png)

## Add an indicator

1. In **Add Master Indicator**, choose the **Category** (target type).
2. Write the **Indicator Description**. Use the click-to-tag helper to mark the number that
   should vary per assignment:
   - Tag a number as the **quantity** — it becomes a `{qty:…}` slot.
   - Tag a number as the **duration** — it becomes a `{duration:value:unit}` slot.
   The list and the tagger keep showing the original number; only the substituted value
   changes per assignment.
3. Choose the **Efficiency Type** (`Quantity-Based`, `Adjectival`, `Client Satisfaction`).
4. Click **Add Indicator**.

## Editing

Use **Edit** on a row to change its description, category, or efficiency type. The preview
under the tagger shows exactly how the sentence will read once quantities/durations are
substituted.

## Why the placeholders matter

When the Dean cascades a quota, or a Chair assigns a per-faculty target, D-IPCR substitutes
the real numbers into the description. This keeps every faculty member's IPCR wording
consistent and removes manual retyping (and typos).

> **Tip:** indicators are scoped to a term. Create them fresh (or copy from a prior term)
> whenever you open a new term.
