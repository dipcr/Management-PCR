# Regular Faculty — Overview

As a Regular Faculty member you **assemble and submit your own IPCR**, then upload evidence
and view your score. Your targets arrive from the Program Chair (Instruction/Support) and the
RET Chair (Research/Extension).

![Faculty dashboard — Overview](/static/img/faculty/00-overview.png)

## Your dashboard

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **My Profile** | Your rank and specialization |
| **Current Term** | The active academic year and semester |
| **My IPCR** | Leads to the IPCR workspace (sidebar → My IPCR) |

## The sidebar

| Group | Item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | **Overview** | This page |
| Performance | **My IPCR** | Assemble, submit, evidence, and view your IPCR |

## What you will do this term

1. Wait for your **Program Chair** (Instruction/Support) and **RET Chair**
   (Research/Extension) to finish allocating targets.
2. Open **My IPCR** and review your targets.
3. Select any **Research** you are eligible for; **Extension** is already assigned.
4. **Submit** your draft IPCR for review.
5. After approval and locking, **upload evidence** (PDF) per target.
6. View your **scored IPCR** and print it.

Continue to [My IPCR](/p/faculty/02-my-ipcr).
