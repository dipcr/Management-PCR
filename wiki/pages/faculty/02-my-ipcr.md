# Regular Faculty — My IPCR

This is where you build and submit your Individual Performance Commitment and Review.

![My IPCR — Draft Mode](/static/img/faculty/02-my-ipcr.png)

## Draft Mode

At the top you will see **Draft Mode** and the sections of your IPCR:

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Strategic Priorities** | Your **Instruction** targets (from the Program Chair) |
| **Support Functions** | Your **Support** targets (from the Program Chair) |
| **Core Functions / RET** | **Research** (self-selected) and **Extension** (assigned) |

## The submission gate

If your chairs have not finished, a banner tells you what is still pending, for example:

> *Draft IPCR Submission Unavailable —*
> *Strategic Priorities (Instruction): Program Chair allocation Pending*
> *Support Functions: Program Chair allocation Pending*
> *Extension Targets: RET Chair configuration for Instructor I Pending*
> *Research Target Pool: RET Chair configuration for Instructor I Pending*

**You cannot submit until every listed item is ready.** This prevents submitting an
incomplete IPCR. If it stays pending, contact the relevant chair.

## The mandatory teaching load

Your IPCR always includes a **Mandatory Default Target** — the Teaching Load (e.g. *21 hours
of Teaching Load*), shown under Strategic Priorities. It is added automatically from the
Admin's teaching-load configuration.

## Building and submitting

1. Review each section's targets and quantities.
2. For **Research**, tick the option(s) you are eligible for from the pool (your RET Chair's
   rank rules decide what you may pick).
3. Confirm the deadlines (durations) shown for each target.
4. Click **Submit IPCR for Review** once the gate clears.

Your draft then goes to review — the **RET Chair first** if you have Research targets, then
the **Program Chair**.

## After review

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *Returned* | The reviewer sent it back — fix and resubmit |
| *Approved* | Your targets are **locked** next — there is no Dean step for targets |
| *Locked / Committed* | Your targets are copied to *committed targets* and **evidence upload opens** |

## Evidence

Once locked, upload a **PDF** per committed target:

1. Open the target and click **Upload Evidence**.
2. Choose a `.pdf` file (max 10 MB).
3. The **Program Chair** **approves** or **returns** it — all regular faculty evidence goes
   through them (the RET Chair no longer verifies evidence). Once every file is reviewed, the
   **Dean** gives a final package sign-off.
4. You can see the readiness status; returned evidence must be re-uploaded.

## Scoring and printing

Once the Dean signs off your verified package, D-IPCR scores each target for **Quantity,
Efficiency and Timeliness**, rolls them into your weighted categories, and produces a **Final
Weighted Rating** with an adjectival band. Open **My IPCR** to view the score and print the
official form.
