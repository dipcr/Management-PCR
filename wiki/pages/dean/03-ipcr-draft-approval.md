# Dean — IPCR Draft Approval

A Program Chair, RET Chair or Dean is a **designated faculty member** with an IPCR of their
own. Unlike regular faculty, a designated faculty member's IPCR is **formulated by the Dean**
rather than self-submitted. This screen is where you do that.

![IPCR Draft Approval](/static/img/dean/03-ipcr-draft-approval.png)

## The Draft IPCR Studio

1. Go to **IPCR Draft Approval**.
2. Pick the designated faculty member to work on.
3. Their IPCR has two tables:
   - **Core Functions** — their own Instruction/teaching share (from the Program Chair's
     allocation plus the mandatory teaching load).
   - **Strategic Priorities & Support Functions** — their oversight/administrative work.
4. Set the quantities and durations, then **issue** the draft. It becomes pre-approved and
   appears in the person's **My IPCR** under *Awaiting Dean Formulation* → issued.

## What the designated faculty member sees

Until you issue the draft, their My IPCR shows:

> *Waiting for the Dean to formulate your Draft IPCR — your Core Functions and Strategic
> Priorities & Support Functions are formulated by the Dean, then issued here pre-approved.*

Once issued, they can view it and proceed to the later phases (evidence, scoring).

## Notes

- Plain **Designated Faculty** have no Target Allocation phase — that menu belongs to the
  Program Chair and RET Chair dashboards. A **chair** who is also a designated faculty member
  can still distribute their own Instruction share in their own Phase 1 before you formulate
  the draft.
- Departmental Oversight rows have no upload slot of their own — their evidence is linked
  automatically from the scoped faculty who did the work.
