# Dean — Department Accomplishment

A read-only view of how each department is doing: the **quota cascaded** versus the
accomplishment **already approved**, so you can see progress without digging into each IPCR.

![Department Accomplishment](/static/img/dean/05-department-accomplishment.png)

## What each row shows

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Departmental Oversight Target** | The cascaded target for the department |
| **Quota** | The cascaded quantity |
| **Verified Accomplished** | Units already approved from submitted evidence |
| **Progress** | Verified ÷ Quota |

This mirrors the equivalent view the Program Chair and RET Chair see for their own scope, but
across the whole college.

## How to use it

- During the term, use it to spot departments that are behind and nudge the chair.
- Before **Final Verification**, use it to sanity-check that evidence has been verified.

> The numbers only count **approved** evidence — pending or returned evidence does not move
> the progress bar.
