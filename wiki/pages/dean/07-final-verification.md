# Dean — Final Verification

The last human gate before an IPCR is locked and scored. Final IPCRs that have cleared the
chair reviews appear here.

![Final Verification](/static/img/dean/07-final-verification.png)

## Approve a final IPCR

1. Go to **Final Verification** (the Overview KPI *Awaiting Final Approval* links here).
2. Review the submitted IPCR — categories, target descriptions, quantities and durations.
3. **Approve** to send it forward to locking/scoring, or **Reject / Return** with a remark.

## What happens after approval

1. The IPCR is **locked**, and its draft targets are copied into **committed targets**.
2. Evidence upload per committed target becomes available.
3. Once evidence is verified, **scoring** computes Q/E/T per target and rolls up to the
   weighted categories, a **Final Weighted Rating** and an **adjectival band**.
4. The **printable IPCR** becomes available.

## Reminders

- The **Awaiting Final Approval** KPI counts both regular and designated faculty; the
  per-department tracker's *Awaiting Your Approval* column counts regular faculty only.
- An approved IPCR cannot be edited — corrections must go through a return.
