# Dean — Final Verification

The **package-level sign-off** — your one and only final approval, and the last human gate
before scoring. It comes **after Evidence Verification**: a faculty member appears here once
every one of their evidence files has been reviewed, and approving records their Final
Weighted Rating.

![Final Verification](/static/img/dean/07-final-verification.png)

## Approve a final IPCR

1. Go to **Final Verification** (the Overview KPI *Awaiting Final Approval* links here).
2. Review the submitted IPCR — categories, target descriptions, quantities, durations, and
   the computed Q/E/T ratings and Final Weighted Rating.
3. **Approve** to record the score, or **Reject / Return** with a remark.

## What happens after approval

1. The targets are marked *Dean Approved* and the **Final Weighted Rating** is recorded.
2. The **printable IPCR** becomes available.

## Reminders

- Targets are **already locked** by the time they reach you — locking happens as soon as the
  Program Chair approves the draft, before evidence upload opens. The Dean does not verify
  targets.
- A package only appears here once **every** evidence file in it has been reviewed, so you
  never sign off on a half-reviewed package.
- The **Awaiting Final Approval** KPI counts both regular and designated faculty; the
  per-department tracker's *Awaiting Your Approval* column counts regular faculty only.
- An approved IPCR cannot be edited — corrections must go through a return.
