# Dean — Target Assignment

Assign **College-Wide / Departmental / RET oversight** targets to designated faculty
(chairs and the Dean). These are the targets that sit under *Strategic Priorities & Support
Functions* on a designated faculty member's IPCR.

![Target Assignment](/static/img/dean/04-target-assignment.png)

## Assign a target

1. Go to **Target Assignment**.
2. Choose the designated faculty member.
3. Tick the College-Wide target(s) to assign and set their quantity and duration.
4. Save.

## Rules to know

- **Departmental / RET Oversight** targets are the person's **whole cascaded quota** — the
  quantity is locked; only the deadline (duration) is editable.
- A target can be both this person's own **Core** work and an **oversight** item; the
  `is_admin_function` flag on the target distinguishes oversight/cascaded work from personal
  teaching, independently of the target's own type.
- College-Wide targets are only assignable once the Dean has cascaded them (see
  [Quota Cascading](/p/dean/02-quota-cascading)).

## Relationship to other screens

- **IPCR Draft Approval** formulates the *whole* draft for a designated faculty member.
- **Target Assignment** attaches individual oversight targets.
- The Program Chair distributes the person's **Instruction** (Core) share separately.
