# Dean — Overview

The Dean is the **second stage of the cascade**: quotas flow from the Admin's indicators,
through the Dean, down to the Program Chairs and Faculty. The Dean also oversees draft
IPCRs for designated faculty and gives final approval.

![Dean dashboard — Overview](/static/img/dean/00-overview.png)

## The sidebar

| Group | Item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | **Overview** | Completion, pending approvals and a per-department tracker |
| Phases | **Quota Cascading** | Push quotas to programs, College-Wide and RET |
| Phases | **IPCR Draft Approval** | Formulate/review designated-faculty drafts (Draft IPCR Studio) |
| Phases | **Target Assignment** | Assign College-Wide/oversight targets to designated faculty |
| Phases | **Department Accomplishment** | Cascaded quota vs approved accomplishment |
| Phases | **Evidence Verification** | Verify evidence packages sent up by chairs |
| Phases | **Final Verification** | Final approval of submitted IPCRs |
| My Performance | **My IPCR** | Your own IPCR (a Dean is also a designated faculty member) |

## Reading the Overview

- **Target Completion** — % of committed target items approved (item-level, not headcount).
- **Awaiting Final Approval** — final IPCRs waiting in **Final Verification**.
- **Not Yet Started** — regular faculty with no draft targets this term.
- **Term Completion Tracker** — for each department: **Total Faculty**, **In Progress**,
  **Awaiting Your Approval**, and **Completed**, plus College-Wide.

> The **Awaiting Your Approval** column covers regular faculty only; the
> **Awaiting Final Approval** KPI is college-wide and also includes designated
> faculty/chairs — so the two numbers will not always match.

## Typical order of work

1. **Quota Cascading** — set each program's quota (and College-Wide / RET).
2. **IPCR Draft Approval** — formulate drafts for designated faculty (chairs, yourself).
3. **Target Assignment** — attach College-Wide / oversight targets.
4. **Department Accomplishment** — monitor distribution vs. accomplishment.
5. **Evidence Verification**, then **Final Verification**.
