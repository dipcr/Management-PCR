# Designated Faculty — Overview

**Designated Faculty** are employees with an accountability role — Program Chair, RET Chair,
Dean — plus plain "Designated Faculty". They log into their own role dashboard **and** have a
personal IPCR, scored with the designated weight table (75% Strategic Priorities & Support
Functions / 25% Core Functions).

![Designated Faculty dashboard](/static/img/designated-faculty/00-overview.png)

## The sidebar

| Group | Item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | **Overview** | Your assigned targets and status |
| Phases | **Target Allocation** | Distribute your own Instruction share (Phase 1) |
| Phases | **Commitments** | Your commitment targets |
| Verification | **Evidence Verification** | Your evidence |
| My Performance | **My IPCR** | The IPCR itself |

## How your IPCR is built

Unlike regular faculty, a designated faculty member's IPCR is **formulated by the Dean**
through the Draft IPCR Studio, then issued to you pre-approved:

> *Waiting for the Dean to formulate your Draft IPCR — your Core Functions and Strategic
> Priorities & Support Functions are formulated by the Dean, then issued here.*

You can still **distribute your own Instruction share to yourself** in **Target Allocation**
while you wait.
