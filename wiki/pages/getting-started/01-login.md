# Logging in

![The D-IPCR sign-in page](/static/img/00-login.png)

1. Open **https://dipcr.yaspartame.site** in your browser.
2. Type your **Corporate Email**.
3. Type your **Password** (the eye icon toggles visibility).
4. Click **Sign In**.

You are taken straight to the dashboard for your role — Admin, Dean, Program Chair,
RET Chair, Designated Faculty or Faculty.

## Which dashboard will I land on?

| Your role | Where you land |
| --- | --- |
| System Administrator | `/admin/` |
| College Dean | `/dean/` |
| Program Chair | `/prog_chair/` |
| RET Chair | `/ret_chair/` |
| Designated Faculty | `/designated/` |
| Regular Faculty | `/faculty/` |

## If you cannot sign in

The login page shows a message explaining why:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Invalid Credentials.* | Wrong email or password | Check spelling; passwords are case-sensitive |
| *Your account claim is pending administrator approval.* | The claim was received but not yet approved | Wait, or ask the Admin to approve it |
| *Your account claim was denied…* | An Admin denied the claim | Contact the Admin; you can claim again |
| *Your account has been deactivated…* | The account was locked | Contact the Admin |

> **Tip:** every page has a **User Manual** link in the sidebar that returns you to the
> in-app help, and a **Logout** button at the bottom of the sidebar.
