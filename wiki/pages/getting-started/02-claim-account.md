# Claiming your account

D-IPCR does not let you create an account from nothing. HR/Admin first adds your profile
to the roster (with your **Employee ID**); you then **claim** that profile and set your own
password. Claims are **reviewed by an administrator** before you can sign in.

![The Claim Your Account page](/static/img/getting-started/02-claim-account.png)

## Step by step

1. Go to **https://dipcr.yaspartame.site/register** — or click **Claim your account** on the
   login page.
2. Enter your **Employee ID Number** exactly as HR recorded it (e.g. `2024-1045`).
3. Enter your **Corporate Email**.
4. Choose a **New password**, then **Confirm password**, meeting the on-screen policy:
   - at least 8 characters
   - one uppercase letter and one lowercase letter
   - one number
   - one special character
   - no spaces
5. Click **Claim Account**.

You will see:

> *Your account claim has been submitted. An administrator must approve it before you can sign in.*

## What happens next

1. Your request appears in the Admin's **Account Claims** queue.
2. The Admin **Approves** it — you can now log in — or **Denies** it.
3. If denied, the request is removed, freeing your Employee ID and email so you can
   claim again with corrected details.

![The Admin's Account Claims queue](/static/img/admin/07-account-claims.png)

## Common problems

| Message | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| *Employee ID Number is not recognized by HR…* | The ID was mistyped, or HR has not added your profile yet |
| *This employee account has already been claimed…* | A credential already exists (claimed, or pending approval) |
| *This corporate email is already registered to another account.* | Use a different email |
